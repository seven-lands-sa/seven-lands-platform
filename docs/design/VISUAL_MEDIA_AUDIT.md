## 1. Executive Summary & Quality Gap Statement

An exhaustive audit of all public routes (`/ar`, `/en`, `/ar/services`, `/ar/services/[slug]`, `/ar/work`, `/ar/about`, `/ar/contact`, `/ar/start-a-project`) reveals that the Seven Lands website was historically constructed as a 100% typographic and SVG-glyph-driven system. 

While the typographic hierarchy (Alexandria for Arabic, Manrope for Latin), micro-interactions, and geometric glyphs are sound, the site completely lacks a **photographic and cinematic layer**.

### Core Deficiency Identified:
1. **Zero Curated Photographic Storytelling:** The `public/` directory contains only vector marks, fonts, and two static OpenGraph images. There are zero editorial photos of Saudi business environments, modern Riyadh architecture (KAFD, Olaya), high-end creative studios, production gear, digital technology workspaces, or brand craft.
2. **Text-Heavy Cognitive Fatigue:** Key routes such as the Services Hub, Service Detail pages, and About page present dense columns of text without visual anchors or editorial pacing.
3. **Absence of Saudi Business Identity Resonance:** Seven Lands is positioned as a premier Saudi marketing, technology, and business-growth company. Without authentic visual grounding in contemporary Saudi commercial excellence, visitors experience an abstract agency rather than an elite Riyadh partner.

## 3. Strict Photography Anti-Patterns & Quality Rules

To preserve brand dignity and prevent cheapening the Seven Lands identity:

1. ❌ **NO Generic Stock Photography:**
   - No Western business models in generic generic offices.
   - No unnatural handshake or thumbs-up poses.
   - No clichéd "people pointing at whiteboard graphs" stock.
2. ❌ **NO Cheap or Distorted AI Imagery:**
   - No unnatural hands, distorted finger joints, or melting backgrounds.
   - No eerie glossy plastic skin or uncanny valley eyes.
   - No generic futuristic purple/cyan neon grids or floating holographic brains.
3. ✅ **MANDATORY Standards for Photographic Assets:**
   - **Authentic Middle Eastern & Saudi Commercial Reality:** Natural Saudi attire (Shemagh / Ghutra / Thobe) depicted with elegance and modern executive dignity where humans appear, alongside cosmopolitan technical environments.
   - **Cinematic Lighting & Depth:** Natural soft daylight, warm architectural accent lighting (3000K), deep ink shadows, high dynamic range.
   - **Material Authenticity:** Real surfaces (travertine stone, honed concrete, brushed bronze, textured paper, anodized aluminum).
   - **Modern Technical Precision:** High-resolution monitors, genuine code syntax, real camera hardware.
